Our story

Nokamabovu Credit Solutions (Pty) Ltd is a 100% Black female-owned, B-BBEE Level 1 credit solutions company, established in 2015 on principles of integrity, efficiency, and client-centered service. Over time we've grown into a trusted partner for public and private institutions, helping them grow through tailored credit and insurance solutions, while also recovering what's already owed — all to the same ethical standard.

We understand that every client relationship calls for sensitivity, professionalism, and strategic engagement — whether we're growing revenue or recovering what's owed. Our sales services connect public and private institutions with tailored credit and insurance solutions to strengthen their portfolios, while our collections division pairs firm recovery strategies with dignity for every debtor, protecting our clients' reputations. Together, these pillars — building revenue and recovering what's due — are backed by ethical, innovative strategies that position us for growth in a market that values integrity and transparency.
