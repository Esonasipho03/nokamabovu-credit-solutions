Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{ last_updated }}

Nokamabovu Credit Solutions (Pty) Ltd (Reg. No. 2015/159073/07) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it, in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

1. Information we collect

  • Website enquiries: your name, email address, phone number, company and message when you use our contact form.
  • Client portfolios: account and contact details of customers that our clients (such as schools, municipalities and healthcare providers) hand over to us for collection, under a written agreement with that client.
  • Calls and messages: call recordings and records of SMS, WhatsApp and email contact, kept for quality, training and compliance purposes.

2. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or prepare a proposal.
  • To collect outstanding accounts on behalf of our clients, and to agree repayment arrangements.
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We only use it for the purpose it was collected for.

3. Who we share it with

We only share personal information with the client who placed the account with us, with service providers who help us operate (for example IT, telephony and messaging providers) under confidentiality agreements, and with authorities where the law requires it.

4. How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to keep personal information secure, including access controls, secure systems and staff confidentiality undertakings. We keep information only for as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, or as the law requires.

5. Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • ask whether we hold personal information about you, and request a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete information that is wrong, out of date or unlawfully held;
  • object to us processing your information in certain circumstances;
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator.
